# ESQET Primary Standard: Quantum Coherence Reference (QCR)

## Device Overview

The Quantum Coherence Reference (QCR) is a metrology-grade primary standard that realizes the ESQET Coherence Constant - the universal minimum phase-noise state of an entangled interferometric resonator.

## Four-Component Architecture

### 1. Dual Optical Lattice Clocks
- Atoms: Strontium (Sr) or Ytterbium (Yb)
- Transition frequency: ~4-5×10¹⁴ Hz
- Stability target: 1×10⁻¹⁸ fractional

### 2. Macroscopic Entanglement Cavity
- Type: Fabry-Perot with squeezed light
- Length: 1 meter
- Squeezing target: ~10 dB

### 3. Superconducting Quantum Loop
- Material: Niobium (Nb)
- Josephson junctions: 1000
- Phase sensitivity: 1×10⁻¹⁸ rad/√Hz

### 4. Gravitational Isolation Stack
- Seismic isolation: LIGO-inspired
- Temperature: 4 K (cryogenic)
- Vibration isolation: 1×10⁶
