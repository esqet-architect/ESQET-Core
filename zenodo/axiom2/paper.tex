\begin{abstract}
The vacuum manifold is $\mathcal{M}_{\text{vac}} = (S^3 \times S^1)/\mathbb{Z}_2$ with $\mathbb{Z}_2$ action $(g,\theta) \sim (-g, \theta+\pi)$. This topology forces meron action $S_{\text{meron}} = 4\pi^2/g^2$ (half the instanton) and universal loop factor $1/(8\pi^2)$. Both are topologically protected by $w_1(\mathcal{M}_{\text{vac}}) \neq 0$.
\end{abstract}
